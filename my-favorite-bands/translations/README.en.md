# 🎸 My Favorite Bands — Hello World Project

> "Without music, life would be a mistake." — Friedrich Nietzsche

A small **"Hello, World!"** style pet project made to practice Markdown and Git.
It doesn't stream any music — it's just a friendly showcase of the bands I listen to on repeat: *Nirvana*, *Deftones*, *Кишлак* and *Хаски*.

---

## Bands

- **Nirvana** — the Aberdeen grunge band that defined the 90s rock sound.
- **Deftones** — Sacramento alternative metal, heavy riffs with a dreamy, shoegaze-like edge.
- **Кишлак** (Maxim Fisenko) — Russian lo-fi rap mixed with punk and folk, raw and provocative.
- **Хаски** (Dmitry Kuznetsov) — Russian rapper from Buryatia, dark and poetic, often compared to Yesenin.

Real promotional photos of the artists aren't included here — they're copyrighted. Original SVG icons are used instead.
